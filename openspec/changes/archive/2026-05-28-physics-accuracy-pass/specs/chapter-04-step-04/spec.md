## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Step 4 — Moving source refutes emission theory, not the ether

Chapter 4 Step 4 ("The moving source") SHALL render a source moving uniformly when it flashes, with the resulting pulse anchored at its **birth point** and expanding at `c` in all directions (no forward bias), using `lm-light-scene` emission-at-birth-point rendering. Narration SHALL frame this as the test of **emission (ballistic) theory** — the idea that light inherits the source's velocity — and SHALL conclude that it does not. Narration SHALL NOT claim that this demo, by itself, disproves a stationary ether, and SHALL NOT present it as the explanation for the Michelson–Morley null result (those are separate threads about motion *through a medium*).

#### Scenario: Pulse anchored at the birth point

- **WHEN** the source flashes while moving and the timeline advances
- **THEN** the pulse circle stays centered on the position where the source was at emission
- **AND** the circle expands at `c` symmetrically (no forward bias, no inherited source velocity)
- **AND** the source dot continues moving past the pulse center

#### Scenario: Narration targets emission theory

- **WHEN** Step 4's narrate beats run
- **THEN** narration identifies the tested idea as light inheriting / being dragged forward by the source's motion (emission/ballistic theory)
- **AND** narration concludes light does not inherit the source's velocity

#### Scenario: Demo is not over-claimed against the ether or Michelson–Morley

- **WHEN** Step 4's narrate beats are inspected
- **THEN** no beat states that the anchored-pulse demo disproves the existence of a stationary ether
- **AND** no beat states that this demo is "why Michelson and Morley saw nothing"
- **AND** the constancy of `c` is attributed to light's nature (self-propagating field), with full demonstration of frame-invariance deferred to a later chapter
